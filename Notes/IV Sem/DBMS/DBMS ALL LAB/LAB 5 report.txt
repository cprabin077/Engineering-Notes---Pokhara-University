1. Display total salary spent for each job category.

Query:
  select category,sum(Salary) from employee inner join
job on employee.job_id = job.job_id group by job.category;

output:
+------------+-------------+
| category   | sum(Salary) |
+------------+-------------+
| designer   |       45000 |
| Hr         |       70000 |
| manager    |       50000 |
| Programmer |       35000 |
| Tester     |       45500 |
+------------+-------------+

query:
   select dept_name,max(salary) from employee group by dept_name;
